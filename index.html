Takeaway

Summary

  • Space Black leads at 62%, and 46% of customers asking for the latest MacBook Pro are ready to choose touchscreen.
  • The M5 Pro chip (52%) and 1TB (63%) are the top configurations across both models.
  • Three in four customers are upgraders. M2 Pro is the most cited prior model, and Lenovo leads the switch-from brands.
  • Creative professionals are the lead audience for the touchscreen. About half of customers ask about affordability options.
  • The lighter design with touchscreen and strong performance are the top features. Fingerprints and app compatibility are the main objections, and price comes up in the comments from half of UAE attended stores.
